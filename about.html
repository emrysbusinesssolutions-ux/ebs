The company

The systems we sell are the systems we run.

EBS builds and operates the same systems it sells. It grew out of Thun Nay Min, a rice-trading business that has operated since 2020, and its shop runs on the system today — through real sales, purchases, stock counts and month-end reports.

That operating experience is what we package for other companies: software that has already done a working day, in a business where the units are bags, pyis, visses and kilos and the internet is not always there.

We are not a reseller passing projects to a distant team. The person who builds the system also supports it, and the trade-offs are ours too.

Principles

What we optimise for

These are the decisions we make when a project gets difficult, not values written for a website.

Offline-first reality

A stable connection here is not something to assume. The system trades on the local network and syncs when a connection returns, instead of stopping when the line drops.

Data ownership

Your database stays on your computer. You can take a copy and export to Excel or CSV. Leaving should not mean losing your history.

Honest scope

If a requirement is outside what the system does well, we say so before the proposal. A smaller honest scope is better than a larger failed one.

Direct support

You talk to the people who build and run the system. There is no call-centre layer between your problem and the person who can fix it.

No lock-in

Excel and CSV export are built in, and the database is yours. We do not hold your history hostage to keep a subscription.

Documentation

Configuration, roles and backup and restore steps are written down for your team, so the system is not a black box when we are not there.

Timeline

Built in production, not in a pitch deck.

There is no funding-round story here. The system grew out of operating a real trading business and fixing what got in the way.

We only date what we can state plainly: the operation started in 2020, the first in-house version followed, and the packaged product is what EBS offers now.
