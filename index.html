Questions

The questions owners ask first.

What does EBS stand for?

EBS stands for Emrys Business Solutions — also written Emrys Business Solution or Emrys BS. It is one company: a small team that builds, deploys and supports business systems.

Where does our data live?

On your own computer. The system runs as a local server on-premise and stores its database there. Phones, tablets and laptops connect over your Wi-Fi. Backups are automated, and you can export or take the database file whenever you want.

What happens when the internet goes down?

Day-to-day work continues. The POS and local network keep operating offline, and changes sync when the connection or power returns. The system is built as an offline-first app, not a website that stops loading.

Can you bring across our Excel sheets and notebooks?

Usually, yes. Data migration is an add-on scoped during discovery: products, units, customers, opening balances and stock. It is quoted with the plan, and we work through it with you rather than dumping a spreadsheet on you. We work through it with you rather than dumping a spreadsheet on you.

How long does setup take?

Typically 2–10 days depending on scope, branches and how clean the starting data is. A single shop on Basic is usually live within a week; a multi-branch rollout takes longer.

Do we own the data and the software?

The data is yours. You can export tables to Excel/CSV and take a copy of the database. The software is licensed to you under the Service Agreement; there is no charge for leaving and no hostage data.

What does support include?

Every tier includes 24/7 support — you can reach us at any time. Response times and additional support work are agreed in the Service Agreement; support beyond the 24/7 channel is a separate service quoted with you. Backups and monitoring run regardless.

Do you work with clients outside Southeast Asia?

Yes. Delivery is remote-first: discovery, deployment, training and support are handled over video and secure remote sessions, so location does not limit us. We work across time zones — tell us where you are and we will schedule around your hours. Pricing and invoices are in USD, with support in English, Burmese, Chinese and Thai.

Can the system run a visa agency's cases end to end?

Yes. A case moves from enquiry to visa issued — applicant and destination, a document checklist with expiry alerts, fees and instalments, embassy or consulate appointments, case notes and an audit trail. Open the visa & immigration demo from the demos page to try the workflow with sample data.

Do you install on site?

No — we don't travel for installation or training. Everything is deployed, configured and supported remotely. On-premise describes where your data lives, on your own computer, not where we are.
