01 — Point of sale

Sell in bags, pyis, visses or kilos without a calculator.

The counter has to keep up with how rice, grain and wholesale goods are actually sold. One item can be priced in several units, and the system does the conversion.

The cashier chooses the item, the unit, the quantity and the price. Stock moves in the base unit, so the ledger stays consistent even when the same bag is sold by the viss. A receipt prints in seconds on a 58 mm thermal printer, and an A4 invoice or a PDF copy is available for the office.

  • Multi-unit selling — bag, pyi, viss and kg with conversion stored in the database
  • Barcode scan and quick item search at the counter
  • 58 mm thermal receipts, A4 invoices and PDF copies
  • Price, discount and payment method recorded on every sale
  • Keeps selling when the network drops, then syncs

Best for: retail counters, rice and grain traders, and wholesale depots that sell in more than one unit.

02 — Inventory & purchasing

Every bag that moves leaves a record.

Purchases, sales, transfers and adjustments post to one stock ledger, so the number in the report can be traced back to the document that changed it.

Receive stock from a supplier, transfer it to another branch, or count the shelves at month end — each action writes a movement. Low-stock views show what to reorder, and unit conversion keeps bags, pyis, visses and kilos consistent across the business.

  • Purchases, suppliers and amounts payable
  • Stock ledger with unit conversion — bag, pyi, viss, kg
  • Low-stock views and stock counts
  • Branch transfers with per-branch permission gates
  • Adjustments for damage, loss and returns

Best for: wholesale and distribution, rice and grain trading, and multi-branch retail.

03 — CRM & receivables

Know who owes you, and when it is due.

Customer and supplier ledgers sit next to the till, so credit is visible while the sale is still being made.

Every credit sale, payment in and payment out posts to the customer's statement. Staff see the balance before extending more credit, and the owner can read total receivables without rebuilding them by hand at the end of the month.

  • Customer and supplier ledgers
  • Statements and unpaid balances
  • Payments in and payments out
  • Sales history by customer
  • Role permissions over who can edit or remove entries

Best for: trading, wholesale and service businesses that sell on credit.

Delivery & deployment

Installed on your premises, supported by the people who build it.

EBS installs the system on a computer you own, on your network. Your team connects from the devices it already uses, and the data does not leave the premises.

On-premise server

The server and database live on a computer at your business. There is no cloud dependency, and the data stays on your premises.

LAN devices, offline-first

Phones, tablets and laptops connect over local Wi-Fi. The app keeps a local database and an outbox, so counters keep selling when the network drops and sync afterwards.

Backups and monitoring

Daily automated backups, weekly restore verification, and health checks every five minutes with a watchdog that restarts the server if it stops.

Data ownership is part of the design. Access is controlled by a role × module × action permission matrix, changes are written to an audit log, and backups are taken automatically. Security overview
