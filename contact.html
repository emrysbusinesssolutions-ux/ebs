Before you write

Questions people ask before the first call

Short answers to the things that come up most often. If yours is not here, ask it in the form — that is what it is for.

Do you work with clients outside Southeast Asia?

Yes. Delivery is remote-first, so location does not limit us. Tell us where you are and your time zone in the form, and we will propose session times around your working hours.

What should I prepare before the first call?

Nothing formal. A rough idea of daily sales, the products or units you sell, how you record stock and who needs access is enough. If you already use a spreadsheet or notebook, keep it nearby.

Does the enquiry form store my details on a server?

No. The site has no server-side form handling. Pressing send builds a pre-filled email in your own mail app, and nothing leaves your device until you send it. If your mail app does not open, copy the draft and paste it into Telegram. The privacy policy explains this in full.

What does it cost?

Published plans are list prices, not quotes. The pricing page lists what each plan includes and the support allowance. The final quotation follows the discovery call and a written scope.

Where does our business data live?

For deployed systems, on your own premises, on a server you control. The product is offline-first, so the shop keeps working if the internet drops. Data ownership, backups and access are covered on the security page.

Not ready to write yet?

The pricing page explains the plans and list prices, and the security overview covers data ownership, backups and support. When you are ready, the form above is the fastest route — or send a short message on Telegram.
