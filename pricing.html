FAQ

Pricing questions, answered plainly

If your question is not here, ask it on the discovery call. We would rather clarify scope than surprise you with a line item later.

Are these prices final?

No. The figures on this page are public list prices, not a quotation. After a discovery call we write the scope and send a final quotation with fixed figures for your business. Nothing is charged from a range.

What does setup include?

Setup covers deploying the system remotely onto your computer, configuring your products, units, receipts and roles, and handing the configuration over to your team on video. Data migration and extra remote training are quoted separately if you need them.

How does upgrading work?

You pay only the difference in setup between your current tier and the new one. Your data, settings and history carry over. The system is upgraded in place, so there is no re-implementation and no data loss.

How is support counted?

Every tier includes 24/7 support — you can reach us at any time. Response times and additional support work are agreed in writing; support beyond the 24/7 channel is a separate service quoted with you.

What are the payment terms?

Invoices are due within 7 days. The quotation states the setup amount and the monthly amount separately, so you can see what is one-time and what recurs.

Where does my data live, and can I leave?

On your own computer. You can export your data to Excel or CSV and you keep the database. The security overview explains the architecture and the no-lock-in commitments.
